Nunchi

Voice:
눈치 보다
nunchi boda
to watch someone's mood and reaction before acting, often with a hint of worry: "상사 눈치를 보다" (to watch the boss's face)
눈치채다
nunchi-chaeda
to catch on, to pick up on something unspoken: "눈치챘어?" (did you notice?)
눈치가 없다
nunchi-ga eopda
to have no nunchi, to be socially oblivious
눈치가 빠르다
nunchi-ga ppareuda
to have quick nunchi
눈치 주다
nunchi juda
to signal, without saying it, that someone is unwelcome or in the way
눈칫밥
nunchit-bap
food eaten under other people's eyes, living uneasily as a dependent
