The PC Bang

Voice:
PC방
pisibang (spoken: 피시방)
"PC room": a shop where you pay to use gaming computers.
자리
jari
"Seat": the place you sit at the computer.
시간
sigan
"Time," and the unit for counting hours: 한 시간 (han sigan) is one hour of use.
라면
ramyeon
"Instant noodles": steamed, fried and dried noodles with a separate soup packet.
게임
geim
"Game": play in which rules are set and players compete to win.
컵라면
keomnamyeon
"Cup ramyeon": noodles in a disposable cup that you eat after pouring in hot water.
충전
chungjeon
"Top-up" or "charging": putting money or time onto your account.
한 시간 할게요
han sigan halgeyo
"I'll do one hour." Say it at the counter.
비회원이에요
bihoewonieyo
"I'm not a member." The word to know at the kiosk or counter.
이 자리 써도 돼요?
i jari sseodo dwaeyo?
"Can I use this seat?" Useful if you want to switch.
컵라면 하나 주세요
keomnamyeon hana juseyo
"One cup ramyeon, please." Said at the counter, or typed on the screen menu.
시간 추가할게요
sigan chugahalgeyo
"I'd like to add time." Say it when your session is about to end.
남은 시간 얼마예요?
nameun sigan eolmayeyo?
"How much time do I have left?"
