Seollal

새해 복 많이 받으세요
sae-hae bok ma-ni ba-deu-se-yo
"Please receive lots of good fortune in the new year." 새해 is the new year, and 복 is good fortune and happiness in life. With close friends, shorten it to 새해 복 많이 받아.
건강하세요
geon-gang-ha-se-yo
"Stay healthy." A common wish for elders and between adults. To a child, an elder says 건강해라 instead.
