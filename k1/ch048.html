The Bookstore

이 책 좀 찾아 주세요.
i chaek jom cha-ja ju-se-yo
"Please find this book for me." Hold up your phone with the cover or the title on it. 제목 (je-mok) is the word for the title of a book, a film or a talk.
한강 작가님 책은 어디 있어요?
han-gang jak-ga-nim chae-geun eo-di i-sseo-yo
"Where are Han Gang's books?" Swap in any author's name. 한강 is only an example here; it is also the name of the Han River, so the 작가님 after it is what tells the clerk you mean a writer.
이 책 재고 있어요?
i chaek jae-go i-sseo-yo
"Do you have this book in stock?" If the answer is 품절이에요 (pum-jeo-ri-e-yo), the item is sold out. You can also ask 이 책 있나요? (i chaek in-na-yo, "do you have this book?").
베스트셀러 코너는 어디예요?
be-seu-teu-sel-leo ko-neo-neun eo-di-ye-yo
"Where is the bestseller corner?" If there is no separate corner, ask 베스트셀러는 어디 있어요? (be-seu-teu-sel-leo-neun eo-di i-sseo-yo, "where are the bestsellers?"). For answers like "on the left", see my direction words (in Korean Words for Travel).
선물 포장되나요?
seon-mul po-jang-doe-na-yo
"Can you gift wrap it?" If you are sure they do, say 선물 포장해 주세요 (seon-mul po-jang-hae ju-se-yo, "please gift wrap it"). Whether wrapping is offered, and whether it costs extra, depends on the shop.
봉투 하나 주세요. 영수증도 주세요.
bong-tu ha-na ju-se-yo. yeong-su-jeung-do ju-se-yo
"One bag, please. And a receipt, please." The first sentence uses 하나, the native number for one. If you cannot find the counter, ask 계산대는 어디예요? (gye-san-dae-neun eo-di-ye-yo, "where is the checkout counter?").
카드로 계산할게요. 포인트 적립도 해 주세요.
ka-deu-ro gye-san-hal-ge-yo. po-in-teu jeong-nip-do hae ju-se-yo
"I will pay by card. Please add the points too." If you do not have a point card, skip the second sentence. If you do, you can say 포인트 카드 있어요 (po-in-teu ka-deu i-sseo-yo, "I have a point card").
