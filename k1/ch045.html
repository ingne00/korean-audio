Book 1: Korean Phrases for Travel · Chapter 45

The Photo Booth

이 프레임으로 할게요
i peu-re-i-meu-ro hal-ge-yo
"I'll go with this frame." Point at the frame on the screen as you say it. 할게요 means "I will do it", which is how you announce a choice.
두 명이에요. 몇 장 나와요?
du myeong-i-e-yo. myeot jang na-wa-yo
"It's two people. How many prints come out?" 두 명 is "two people" and 몇 장 is "how many sheets". For the number of people, use the native numbers from my number words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel): 한 명, 두 명, 세 명, 네 명.
카드 돼요?
ka-deu dwae-yo
"Can I pay by card?" Literally "does card work?" My suggestion: ask before you start.
다시 찍을 수 있어요?
da-si jji-geul su i-sseo-yo
"Can I shoot again?" A word for a retake is 재촬영 (jae-chwal-yeong), so you may also say 재촬영되나요? Retake rules may differ by booth.
사진은 어디서 받아요?
sa-ji-neun eo-di-seo ba-da-yo
"Where do I get the photos?" It means the printed strip. 어디서 is a short form of 어디에서, "from where". If the answer is a pointing hand, my direction words (in Korean Words for Travel) help you follow it.
QR로 원본 받을 수 있어요?
kyu-al-lo won-bon ba-deul su i-sseo-yo
"Can I get the original file by QR?" QR is read kyu-al (큐알), so the first word sounds like kyu-al-lo. 원본 is the word Harufilm's page uses for the original files you take by QR.
QR이 안 돼요
kyu-a-ri an dwae-yo
"The QR isn't working." Hold up your phone and the printed strip as you say it, and staff can see the problem at once.
