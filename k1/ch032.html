The Convenience Store

봉투 하나 주세요
bong-tu ha-na ju-se-yo
"One bag, please." Say it before the last item is scanned, because bags are a paid extra and the cashier will add it to the total.
이거 데워 주세요
i-geo de-wo ju-se-yo
"Please heat this up." Hold up the lunch box or rice triangle you want warmed, and the cashier will usually take it to the microwave behind the counter.
젓가락 주세요
jeot-ga-rak ju-se-yo
"Chopsticks, please." For a spoon, swap in 숟가락 (sut-ga-rak). Handy when your food is not the kind that comes with its own set.
카드로 계산할게요
ka-deu-ro gye-san-hal-ge-yo
"I'll pay by card." Useful when the cashier asks 결제 어떻게 하세요? or just looks at you and waits. Insert or tap the card on the terminal they point to.
티머니로 계산할게요
ti-meo-ni-ro gye-san-hal-ge-yo
"I'll pay with T-money." Say it before they ring you up so they choose the right payment type, then tap the card on the reader.
라면 조리기 사용법 알려 주세요
ra-myeon jo-ri-gi sa-yong-beop al-lyeo ju-se-yo
"Please show me how to use the ramyeon cooker." Point at the machine while you say it. Staff are used to this question.
택스리펀드 되나요?
taek-seu-ri-peon-deu doe-na-yo
"Can I get a tax refund here?" Show your passport only if the store has a Tax Free or Tax Refund sign. 되나요 means "is it possible?".
