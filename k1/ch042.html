Hanbok Rental

두 시간 대여하고 싶어요
du si-gan dae-yeo-ha-go si-peo-yo
"I'd like to rent it for two hours." 대여 is "rental", and many shops also write the loanword 렌탈 on their boards. Hours use native numbers with 시간: 한 시간, 두 시간, 세 시간, 네 시간. Prices and time options vary by shop, so follow up with 두 시간에 얼마예요? ("how much for two hours?"). For more time words, see my time words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel).
이거 입어 봐도 돼요?
i-geo i-beo bwa-do dwae-yo
"Can I try this on?" 입다 is "to wear", and -어 보다 means "to try doing". Pick the jacket and skirt (or trousers) as a set. If you plan to use the hanbok for free palace entry, the official hanbok guideline asks for a jacket (저고리) with a wrap collar plus a skirt or trousers. To ask for a shade, point and say 이 색으로 주세요 ("this color, please").
좀 더 큰 거 있어요?
jom deo keun geo i-sseo-yo
"Do you have a slightly bigger one?" 좀 더 is "a bit more", 큰 is "big" and 거 is "one" or "thing". For smaller, say 좀 더 작은 거 있어요? (jom deo ja-geun geo i-sseo-yo). Skirts wrap and tie, so size matters most for the jacket sleeves and the skirt length; a skirt that drags on the ground gets dirty on gravel paths.
머리 장식도 포함이에요?
meo-ri jang-sik-do po-ha-mi-e-yo
"Is the hair accessory included?" 머리 is "hair" or "head", 장식 is "decoration", and 포함 is "included". Some rental shops list hair styling, accessories or a small bag separately from the basic outfit. Ask before you pick things up from the basket, and use the same pattern for anything else: 가방도 포함이에요? ("is the bag included too?").
짐 좀 맡겨도 돼요?
jim jom mat-gyeo-do dwae-yo
"Can I leave my bag here?" 짐 is luggage or belongings, and 맡기다 is "to leave something in someone's care". Ask whether the shop can keep your own clothes and bag while you are out, and ask about a large suitcase before you arrive. Keep your phone, cards and passport with you.
몇 시까지 반납해야 돼요?
myeot si-kka-ji ban-na-pae-ya dwae-yo
"By what time do I have to return it?" 반납 is "returning" a borrowed item, and -해야 돼요 is "have to". Write the time down or take a photo of the slip. Shops differ on when the clock starts, so check that too: 언제부터 시간 계산해요? ("from when do you count the time?").
시간 연장할 수 있어요?
si-gan yeon-jang-hal su i-sseo-yo
"Can I extend the time?" 연장 is "extension". Ask by phone or message before your time runs out, not after. Shops differ on how they charge for extra time and late returns, so ask at the start: 늦으면 추가 요금 있어요? (neu-jeu-myeon chu-ga yo-geum i-sseo-yo, "is there an extra charge if I'm late?").
