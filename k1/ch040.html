Book 1: Korean Phrases for Travel · Chapter 40

Tourist Information Centers

Voice:
지도 하나 주세요
ji-do ha-na ju-se-yo
"Could I have a map, please?" Literally "give me one map". Point at the rack or the counter. Whether a given desk has paper maps is for the staff to answer, so if the answer is no, move on to line 2.
명동은 어디예요?
myeong-dong-eun eo-di-ye-yo
"Where is Myeongdong?" Swap in any place name: 남산타워는 (nam-san-ta-wo-neun, "Namsan Tower") or 광화문은 (gwang-hwa-mu-neun, "Gwanghwamun"). After a name that ends in a vowel, use 는 instead of 은. Pointing at the map and saying 여기는 어디예요? (yeo-gi-neun eo-di-ye-yo, "where is this place?") works too.
가 볼 만한 곳을 추천해 주세요
ga bol man-han go-seul chu-cheon-hae ju-se-yo
"Please recommend a place worth visiting." The Standard Korean Dictionary defines 추천하다 (推薦하다) as introducing something that suits a given condition, with the recommender taking responsibility. For food, change the first part to 먹을 만한 곳을 (meo-geul man-han go-seul, "a place worth eating at"), and add 이 근처에서 (i geun-cheo-e-seo, "near here") at the start to narrow it down.
거기까지 지하철로 갈 수 있어요?
geo-gi-kka-ji ji-ha-cheol-lo gal su i-sseo-yo
"Can I get there by subway?" Put the place name before 까지 to replace 거기: 남산타워까지 (nam-san-ta-wo-kka-ji, "to Namsan Tower"). Swap 지하철로 for 버스로 (beo-seu-ro, "by bus") or 걸어서 (geo-reo-seo, "on foot"). For station words such as exit and platform, see my subway words (in Korean Words for Travel).
몇 시까지 해요?
myeot si-kka-ji hae-yo
"Until what time are you open?" Literally "until what time do you do it?", where 해요 stands in for "are you open". Put the place first: 안내소는 몇 시까지 해요? (an-nae-so-neun myeot si-kka-ji hae-yo, "until what time is the information desk open?"). For the opening time, ask 몇 시에 열어요? (myeot si-e yeo-reo-yo). The answer will use native numbers for the hour, as in my number words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel).
영어 할 수 있는 분 계세요?
yeong-eo hal su in-neun bun gye-se-yo
"Is there someone who can speak English?" 분 is the polite word for "person" and 계세요 is the polite "to be there". Swap 영어 for 일본어 (il-bo-neo, Japanese) or 중국어 (jung-gu-geo, Chinese). To ask for printed help, say 영어 지도 있어요? (yeong-eo ji-do i-sseo-yo, "do you have a map in English?").
영어 통역을 부탁드립니다
yeong-eo tong-yeo-geul bu-tak-deu-rim-ni-da
"English interpretation, please." Use it when a call or chat connects to the 1330 helpline and you need to continue in English. 통역 (通譯) means putting words across so that people who do not share a language can understand each other (Open Dictionary), and 통역기 is an interpreting device.
