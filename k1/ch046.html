The Hair Salon

내일 오후에 예약 가능해요?
nae-il o-hu-e ye-yak ga-neung-hae-yo
"Is a booking possible tomorrow afternoon?" Use it on the phone or in a Naver or Instagram message. Swap 내일 (nae-il, tomorrow) for 오늘 (o-neul, today) or a weekday. Once you have booked online, say 네이버로 예약했어요 (ne-i-beo-ro ye-ya-kae-sseo-yo, "I booked through Naver") at the front desk.
조금만 다듬어 주세요
jo-geum-man da-deu-meo ju-se-yo
"Please just trim it a little." FOHO lists this as the line to keep your length. 다듬다 means to tidy up. For a bigger change, 짧게 잘라 주세요 (jjal-ge jal-la ju-se-yo) means "please cut it short".
2센티만 잘라 주세요
i-sen-ti-man jal-la ju-se-yo
"Please cut just 2 centimeters." 센티 is the everyday short form of centimeter, and the number before it is Sino-Korean: 1센티 일, 2센티 이, 3센티 삼, 5센티 오, as in my number words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel). Your stylist may ask 얼마나 자를까요? (eol-ma-na ja-reul-kka-yo, "how much shall I cut?"). A landmark works too: 어깨까지 (eo-kkae-kka-ji, "up to the shoulders").
앞머리는 자르지 마세요
am-meo-ri-neun ja-reu-ji ma-se-yo
"Please do not cut the bangs." 앞머리 means bangs or fringe, literally "front hair". To ask for them, say 앞머리 잘라 주세요 (am-meo-ri jal-la ju-se-yo), and add 눈썹 길이로 (nun-sseop gi-ri-ro, "to eyebrow length") if you want a length.
염색하고 싶어요
yeom-sae-ka-go si-peo-yo
"I would like to dye my hair." For a perm, say 펌하고 싶어요 (peom-ha-go si-peo-yo). FOHO lists 다운펌 (da-un-peom), a perm that flattens hair at the sides, as a common men's add-on. Hair length changes the price of both, which is the 기장 추가 charge from above.
이 사진처럼 해 주세요
i sa-jin-cheo-reom hae ju-se-yo
"Please do it like this photo." FOHO calls the photo the single best tool and suggests front, side and back shots. The cut version is 이 사진처럼 잘라 주세요 (i sa-jin-cheo-reom jal-la ju-se-yo).
시작하기 전에 가격 알려 주세요
si-ja-ka-gi jeo-ne ga-gyeok al-lyeo ju-se-yo
"Please tell me the price before we start." Creatrip warns that the final cost can differ from what you saw when booking, because length, hair quality and stylist level change it. Add 샴푸 포함이에요? (syam-pu po-ha-mi-e-yo, "is shampoo included?") and 추가 비용 있어요? (chu-ga bi-yong i-sseo-yo, "are there extra charges?").
