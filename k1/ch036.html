Tax-Free Shopping and Tax Refunds

택스 리펀드 돼요?
taek-seu ri-peon-deu dwae-yo
"Do you do tax refunds?" Clerks use the English loanword more than any Korean term, so this is the line they expect. Ask before the clerk scans your items. The refund is for visitors who are not residents of Korea; the VisitKorea tax refund guide says foreigners staying less than six months are eligible, while people working in Korea are not.
여권 여기 있어요
yeo-gwon yeo-gi i-sseo-yo
"Here is my passport." The shop checks your eligibility through your passport (VisitKorea), so carry the real passport when you plan to shop, not a photo of it. Open it to the photo page before you pass it across.
즉시 환급돼요?
jeuk-si hwan-geup-dwae-yo
"Can I get the refund right away?" 즉시 환급 is the immediate refund: the tax comes off the price at the till, so you pay less and have nothing to claim later. The VisitKorea guide gives the limits: a single purchase under 1 million won, and 5 million won or less in total for the trip. Not every tax-free shop offers it, so ask.
같이 계산해 주세요
ga-chi gye-san-hae ju-se-yo
"Please ring them up together." A purchase has to reach 15,000 won to qualify (VisitKorea). Two small items rung up as one purchase can clear that line where each alone would not. 계산 is "paying the bill".
환급 영수증도 주세요
hwan-geup yeong-su-jeung-do ju-se-yo
"Please give me the refund receipt too." If you did not get the immediate refund, the shop gives you a refund slip along with the normal receipt. Its formal name is 환급전표 (hwan-geup-jeon-pyo, "refund voucher"), but at a counter 환급 영수증 or 택스 리펀드 영수증 is what people say. Keep it flat in your passport cover; you need it at departure.
세관 확인은 어디서 받아요?
se-gwan hwa-gi-neun eo-di-seo ba-da-yo
"Where do I get the customs check?" At departure you show the goods, the refund slips and your passport to customs for export confirmation, and the goods must leave Korea within three months of purchase (VisitKorea). Ask this at the airport information desk; my information desk phrases chapter has more lines for that counter. My suggestion: get the check done before you hand over any bag that holds the items.
환급 창구가 어디예요?
hwan-geup chang-gu-ga eo-di-ye-yo
"Where is the refund counter?" After customs, you collect the refund at a staffed counter or a self-service kiosk in the airport (VisitKorea). If you see machines, the word is 키오스크 (ki-o-seu-keu).
