Hotel Check-In and Check-Out

체크인하려고요
che-keu-in-ha-ryeo-go-yo
"I'd like to check in." The -려고요 ending means "I'm here to" and is how Koreans state their business at a counter. Follow it with the name on the booking: OO 이름으로 예약했어요 ("I booked under the name OO"). Show your passport and booking screen at the same time. The hotel words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel) has the counter vocabulary such as 예약 (booking) and 여권 (passport).
짐 좀 맡길 수 있을까요?
jim jom mat-gil su i-sseul-kka-yo
"Could I leave my bags?" Use it before check-in or after checkout. 맡기다 means to leave something in someone's care. Many hotels will keep luggage at the desk or in a storage room; ask, because policies vary. When you come back, say 짐 찾으러 왔어요 ("I've come to pick up my bags"). If the hotel cannot help, the luggage locker chapter covers station lockers.
조식은 몇 시부터예요?
jo-si-geun myeot si-bu-teo-ye-yo
"What time does breakfast start?" 조식 is the hotel word for breakfast (at home people say 아침). Change 부터 (from) to 까지 (until) to ask when it ends: 몇 시까지예요? Also useful: 조식 포함이에요? ("Is breakfast included?"). For hours and times, see the time words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel).
와이파이 비밀번호가 뭐예요?
wa-i-pa-i bi-mil-beon-ho-ga mwo-ye-yo
"What's the Wi-Fi password?" 비밀번호 is a password or PIN. Many hotels print it on the key card holder or a card in the room, so check there first.
수건 좀 더 주시겠어요?
su-geon jom deo ju-si-ge-sseo-yo
"Could I have some more towels?" Swap 수건 for what you need: 휴지 (toilet paper), 생수 (bottled water), 베개 (pillow), 이불 (blanket). Say it at the desk or by phone from the room; for the phone, start with your room number: OO호인데요 ("This is room OO"). 호 is the counter for room numbers.
체크아웃 좀 늦게 할 수 있을까요?
che-keu-a-ut jom neut-ge hal su i-sseul-kka-yo
"Could I check out a little later?" Late checkout depends on the hotel and on how full it is, and some hotels charge for it, so ask the evening before. Add the time you want with 까지: 12시까지 괜찮을까요? ("Would noon be all right?"). If the answer is a fee, the clerk will say 추가 요금이 있어요 ("there is an extra charge").
체크아웃할게요
che-keu-a-u-tal-ge-yo
"I'll check out now." Put the key card on the counter as you say it. The clerk may ask 미니바 이용하셨어요? ("Did you use the minibar?"); answer 아니요 or name what you took. If you want a receipt, ask 영수증 주세요.
