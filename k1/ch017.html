Calling a Kakao T Taxi

제가 부른 택시예요
je-ga bu-reun taek-si-ye-yo
"This is the taxi I called." Useful when a driver pulls up and looks unsure whether you're the passenger who booked.
여기서 잠깐만 기다려 주세요
yeo-gi-seo jam-kkan-man gi-da-ryeo ju-se-yo
"Please wait here a moment." Handy if you need thirty seconds to grab a bag or a friend before the ride actually starts.
현금으로 낼게요
hyeon-geu-meu-ro nael-ge-yo
"I'll pay in cash." Confirms out loud that you will pay the driver directly, in case the driver is unsure how you chose to pay.
여기서 세워 주세요
yeo-gi-seo se-wo ju-se-yo
"Please stop here." Useful when the app's pin lands a few meters off from where you actually want to get out.
트렁크 좀 열어 주세요
teu-reong-keu jom yeo-reo ju-se-yo
"Please open the trunk." Handy when you have luggage and the trunk stays closed.
취소하고 싶어요
chwi-so-ha-go si-peo-yo
"I want to cancel." Useful if the driver calls you or you reach them and need to say you are canceling. The cancellation itself is done in the app with the 호출취소 (ho-chul-chwi-so, cancel request) button, and a cancellation fee can apply after a driver is assigned.
영수증 주세요
yeong-su-jeung ju-se-yo
"Receipt, please." Worth asking for if you paid cash and need proof for an expense report or a lost-item claim later.
