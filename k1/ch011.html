Book 1: Korean Phrases for Travel · Chapter 11

The Airport Taxi Stand

택시 어디서 타요?
taek-si eo-di-seo ta-yo?
"Where do I catch a taxi?"
여기 가 주세요
yeo-gi ga ju-se-yo
"Please go here" (while pointing at the screen).
트렁크 좀 열어 주세요
teu-reong-keu jom yeo-reo ju-se-yo
"Please open the trunk."
카드 돼요?
ka-deu dwae-yo?
"Can I pay by card?"
얼마 나와요?
eol-ma na-wa-yo?
Loosely, "how much does it come to?"
영수증 주세요
yeong-su-jeung ju-se-yo
"Receipt, please."
여기서 내려 주세요
yeo-gi-seo nae-ryeo ju-se-yo
"Let me off here."
