Book 1: Korean Phrases for Travel · Chapter 52

Lost and Found

지하철에서 가방을 놓고 내렸어요
ji-ha-cheo-re-seo ga-bang-eul no-ko nae-ryeo-sseo-yo
"I left my bag on the subway." The frame is place + 에서 + thing + 을/를 + 놓고 내렸어요, which literally means "put it down and got off". Swap in 택시에서 휴대폰을 (taek-si-e-seo hyu-dae-po-neul, "my phone in a taxi") or 버스에서 지갑을 (beo-seu-e-seo ji-ga-beul, "my wallet on a bus").
어제 저녁 8시쯤 2호선에서 내렸어요
eo-je jeo-nyeok yeo-deol-si jjeum i-ho-seo-ne-seo nae-ryeo-sseo-yo
"I got off a Line 2 train around 8 p.m. yesterday." 쯤 means "around". Hours use the native numbers, as in the number words (in Korean Words for Travel).
카페에 휴대폰을 두고 왔어요
ka-pe-e hyu-dae-po-neul du-go wa-sseo-yo
"I left my phone at the cafe." In everyday speech, use 놓고 내렸어요 for something left in a vehicle and 두고 왔어요 ("left it and came") for something left at a place you already walked away from. 핸드폰 (haen-deu-pon) is just as common as 휴대폰. Both are everyday words for a cell phone (휴대 전화).
혹시 분실물 들어온 거 있어요?
hok-si bun-sil-mul deu-reo-on geo i-sseo-yo
"Has any lost item come in, by any chance?" 혹시 softens the question the way "by any chance" does. 분실물 is the word on counter signs and on the official pages, and it means an item someone has lost. Staff may ask back 어떤 거예요? (eo-tteon geo-ye-yo, "what kind of item?").
검은색 작은 가방이에요
geo-meun-saek ja-geun ga-bang-i-e-yo
"It's a small black bag." Say the color and size first, then one detail: 안에 우산이 있어요 (a-ne u-sa-ni i-sseo-yo, "there is an umbrella inside"). Colors: 흰색 (huin-saek, white), 빨간색 (ppal-gan-saek, red), 파란색 (pa-ran-saek, blue), 갈색 (gal-saek, brown). Big is 큰 (keun).
찾으면 이 번호로 전화 주세요
cha-jeu-myeon i beon-ho-ro jeon-hwa ju-se-yo
"If you find it, please call this number." Show the number on paper or on your phone screen. Say it with Sino-Korean digits, and say 0 as 공 (gong): 010-1234-5678 is 공일공 일이삼사 오육칠팔 (gong-il-gong i-ri-sam-sa o-yuk-chil-pal). That is an example, not a real number. To ask staff to ring your phone, say 제 번호로 전화 한 번 걸어 주시겠어요? (je beon-ho-ro jeon-hwa han beon geo-reo ju-si-ge-sseo-yo, "could you call my number once?").
찾으러 어디로 가면 돼요?
cha-jeu-reo eo-di-ro ga-myeon dwae-yo
"Where should I go to pick it up?" Staff may name a station lost-and-found center (분실물센터, bun-sil-mul-sen-teo) or a police station (경찰서, gyeong-chal-seo). Follow up with 뭐 가져가야 해요? (mwo ga-jeo-ga-ya hae-yo, "what do I need to bring?"), because ID rules differ by place.
