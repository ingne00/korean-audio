The Movie Theater

일곱 시 반 걸로 주세요.
il-gop si ban geol-lo ju-se-yo
"The 7:30 one, please." Say the film title first, or point at it on the screen, then the time. 반 means "half", so 일곱 시 반 is 7:30, and 걸로 is a short form of 것으로, "that one". A 19:30 start is 저녁 일곱 시 반 (jeo-nyeok il-gop si ban, "seven thirty in the evening"). For numbers in general, see my number words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel).
일반 두 명, 청소년 한 명이요.
il-ban du myeong, cheong-so-nyeon han myeong-i-yo
"Two regular fares and one youth fare, please." 일반 and 청소년 are the two fare labels in CGV's Culture Day fares; CGV's FAQ (청소년 요금 이용 기준) defines 청소년 as a person under 19 under the Youth Protection Act, counted from January 1 of the birth year regardless of birthday; people who reach 19 on January 1 of that year are excluded. If everyone pays the regular fare, 두 명이요 (du myeong-i-yo, "two people, please") is enough. To count tickets instead of people, use 장: 두 장 주세요 (du jang ju-se-yo, "two tickets, please").
자막으로 주세요.
ja-ma-geu-ro ju-se-yo
"With subtitles, please." Swap in 더빙으로 (deo-bing-eu-ro, "dubbed") for a dubbed screening. 자막 (字幕) is text shown on the screen of a film or TV program, such as titles, roles, characters' dialogue and explanations.
붙어 있는 자리로 주세요.
bu-teo in-neun ja-ri-ro ju-se-yo
"Seats next to each other, please." To ask about a spot, say 뒤쪽 가운데 자리 있어요? (dwi-jjok ga-un-de ja-ri i-sseo-yo, "is there a seat in the middle toward the back?"). Seat labels vary, so read the row and number on your ticket.
3관은 어디예요?
sam-gwa-neun eo-di-ye-yo
"Where is screen 3?" 관 names a screening room; it is the last part of 상영관. CGV's showtime list prints it after the number (13관, 7관), and a 상영관 is a room where films are shown to an audience. If a staff member answers 저쪽이에요 (jeo-jjo-gi-e-yo), that means "it's over there".
팝콘하고 콜라 하나씩 주세요.
pap-kon-ha-go kol-la ha-na-ssik ju-se-yo
"One popcorn and one cola, please." 하나씩 means "one each". Another option is 콤보 하나 주세요 (kom-bo ha-na ju-se-yo, "one combo, please"): CGV's FAQ writes 매점 콤보 for the snack-stand sets.
환불하고 싶은데요.
hwan-bul-ha-go si-peun-de-yo
"I'd like a refund." The ending -고 싶은데요 makes it a soft request. To change the time, say 시간을 바꾸고 싶은데요 (si-ga-neul ba-kku-go si-peun-de-yo, "I'd like to change the time"). CGV and Megabox both say a change means canceling and booking again. 환불 (還拂) means returning money already paid.
