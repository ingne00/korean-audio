Book 1: Korean Phrases for Travel · Chapter 22

Renting a Car

Voice:
예약번호는 이거예요. 확인해 주세요.
ye-yak-beon-ho-neun i-geo-ye-yo. hwa-gin-hae ju-se-yo.
"This is my reservation number. Please check it." Have the number ready (my suggestion). If the clerk asks you to read it out, my number words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel) covers the numbers.
여권하고 국제운전면허증 여기 있어요.
yeo-gwon-ha-go guk-je-un-jeon-myeon-heo-jeung yeo-gi i-sseo-yo.
"Here are my passport and international driving permit." 하고 means "and", and 여기 있어요 means "here you are". Lotte's page says to hand over the original IDP, and SK's FAQ says the name on the passport and the IDP must match.
자차보험은 어떤 게 있어요?
ja-cha-bo-heo-meun eo-tteon ge i-sseo-yo?
"What kinds of own-car insurance are there?" 자차 means the car itself, so this asks about cover for damage to the rental car. Once you decide, 완전면책으로 할게요 (wan-jeon-myeon-chae-geu-ro hal-ge-yo) means "I'll take the full waiver", the zero-excess plan on Lotte's list.
반납할 때 기름은 가득 채워야 돼요?
ban-na-pal ttae gi-reu-meun ga-deuk chae-wo-ya dwae-yo?
"Do I have to fill the tank when I return it?" Lotte's page says full at return, as at pickup (details above). For the time, ask 반납은 몇 시까지예요? (ban-na-beun myeot si-kka-ji-ye-yo, "Until what time is the return?"). Return times vary, so ask. My time words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel) helps you understand the answer.
여기 흠집이 있는데요. 확인해 주세요.
yeo-gi heum-ji-bi in-neun-de-yo. hwa-gin-hae ju-se-yo.
"There's a scratch here. Please check it." 흠집 covers a scratch or any other mark. Lotte's page puts this walk-around before you sign, and says the renter pays for any damage during the rental, no matter who is at fault. My suggestion: point at each mark, and ask 사진 찍어도 돼요? (sa-jin jji-geo-do dwae-yo, "May I take photos?").
이 차에 하이패스 있어요?
i cha-e ha-i-pae-seu i-sseo-yo?
"Does this car have Hi-Pass?" Then ask 통행료는 어떻게 내요? (tong-haeng-nyo-neun eo-tteo-ke nae-yo, "How do I pay the tolls?").
차 반납이요. 확인 부탁드려요.
cha ban-na-bi-yo. hwa-gin bu-tak-deu-ryeo-yo.
"I'm returning the car. Please check it." Lotte's order is: key to staff, a check of the car and fuel together, then settling extra charges (the word for settling up is 정산). If the branch is closed, ask about the Key Drop Box (무인키박스) described above. If you left something behind, see lines 3 and 4 of my lost and found phrases.
