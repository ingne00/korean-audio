Renting a Ddareungi Bike

따릉이 어떻게 빌려요?
tta-reung-i eo-tteo-ke bil-lyeo-yo
"How do I rent a Ddareungi bike?" Mostly useful if you're asking a passerby to point you at the app rather than a staff member, since there usually isn't one nearby.
자전거 대여소가 어디예요?
ja-jeon-geo dae-yeo-so-ga eo-di-ye-yo
"Where is the bike rental station?" 자전거 alone means bicycle, useful even without the rest of the sentence if you're pointing at your phone map.
이 자전거 고장 났어요
i ja-jeon-geo go-jang na-sseo-yo
"This bike is broken." Report it through the app if you can, but this sentence works if a staff member from the operation center happens to be nearby doing maintenance.
헬멧 있어요?
hel-met i-sseo-yo
"Do you have a helmet?" A loanword sentence, and a fair one, since helmet rental at the stations is not something to count on and most riders go without.
여기 반납해도 돼요?
yeo-gi ban-na-pae-do dwae-yo
"Can I return it here?" Worth asking out loud to a local if the dock looks half full and you're not sure there's an open slot, since a full dock will refuse the return in the app.
자전거 도로가 어디예요?
ja-jeon-geo do-ro-ga eo-di-ye-yo
"Where is the bike lane?" Dedicated bike lanes are marked with bicycle symbols and signs, and staying on them is both safer and less likely to draw an annoyed look from drivers.
한강까지 얼마나 걸려요?
han-gang-kka-ji eol-ma-na geol-lyeo-yo
"How long does it take to get to the Han River?" A genuinely useful question, since the riverside path is the single most popular route and most stations are a short ride from some access point to it.
