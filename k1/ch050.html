Book 1: Korean Phrases for Travel · Chapter 50

The Laundromat

카드로 결제할 수 있어요?
ka-deu-ro gyeol-je-hal su i-sseo-yo
"Can I pay by card?" If the machine shows only a coin slot, ask 동전만 돼요? (dong-jeon-man dwae-yo, "is it coins only?"). 동전 means coin and 결제 means payment.
이거 어떻게 시작해요?
i-geo eo-tteo-ke si-ja-kae-yo
"How do I start this?" Point at the machine. The usual answer is: close the door, choose the course, then pay. 시작 means start.
세탁이랑 건조는 얼마나 걸려요?
se-ta-gi-rang geon-jo-neun eol-ma-na geol-lyeo-yo
"How long do the wash and the dry take?" 세탁 is washing and 건조 is drying. Cleantopia says about 60 minutes for both together, but the screen shows the real time for your course.
세제는 어디 있어요?
se-je-neun eo-di i-sseo-yo
"Where is the detergent?" If the shop adds it automatically, ask 세제가 자동으로 들어가요? (se-je-ga ja-dong-eu-ro deu-reo-ga-yo, "does the detergent go in automatically?").
돈을 넣었는데 작동이 안 돼요
do-neul neo-eon-neun-de jak-dong-i an dwae-yo
"I put money in, but it does not work." Add 기계가 멈췄어요 (gi-gye-ga meom-chwo-sseo-yo, "the machine stopped") or 기계가 돈을 먹었어요 (gi-gye-ga do-neul meo-geo-sseo-yo, "the machine ate my money"). Then look for the contact number posted in the shop. That last step is my advice, not an operator rule.
건조기도 있어요?
geon-jo-gi-do i-sseo-yo
"Is there a dryer too?" Also useful: 건조기는 어디 있어요? (geon-jo-gi-neun eo-di i-sseo-yo, "where is the dryer?"). Cleantopia says its dryers run a 30-minute quick dry.
동전 교환기 어디 있어요?
dong-jeon gyo-hwan-gi eo-di i-sseo-yo
"Where is the coin changer?" Look around the room for a changer, and if there is none, a convenience store can break a bill. See the “Convenience Store” chapter. Say 천 원을 동전으로 바꿔 주세요 (cheon wo-neul dong-jeo-neu-ro ba-kkwo ju-se-yo, "please change 1,000 won into coins") to a clerk.
