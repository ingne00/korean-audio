The Post Office

박스 하나 주세요
bak-seu ha-na ju-se-yo
"One box, please." If you want to ask first, say 박스 있어요? (bak-seu i-sseo-yo, "do you have boxes?").
미국으로 보내 주세요
mi-gu-geu-ro bo-nae ju-se-yo
"To the United States, please." Literally "please send it to America." Swap 미국 (mi-guk, U.S.) for 일본 (il-bon, Japan), 영국 (yeong-guk, U.K.), 캐나다 (kae-na-da, Canada) or 호주 (ho-ju, Australia). For a single postcard, say 엽서 한 장, 미국으로 보내고 싶어요 (yeop-seo han jang, mi-gu-geu-ro bo-nae-go si-peo-yo, "one postcard, I want to send it to the U.S.").
안에 책이랑 옷이 들어 있어요
a-ne chae-gi-rang o-si deu-reo i-sseo-yo
"Inside there are books and clothes." The clerk may ask 안에 뭐 들어 있어요? (a-ne mwo deu-reo i-sseo-yo, "what is inside?") or 내용물이 뭐예요? (nae-yong-mu-ri mwo-ye-yo, "what are the contents?"). Swap in 책 (chaek, books), 옷 (ot, clothes), 신발 (sin-bal, shoes) or 서류 (seo-ryu, documents). Say the real items and give the value on the form.
EMS로 할게요
i-em-e-seu-ro hal-ge-yo
"I'll go with EMS." Koreans read the letters aloud, so EMS is 이엠에스 (i-em-e-seu). For the slow route, say 배편으로 할게요 (bae-pyeo-neu-ro hal-ge-yo, "I'll go by ship") or use Korea Post's own word, 선편으로 할게요 (seon-pyeo-neu-ro hal-ge-yo). To compare first, ask 어느 게 더 빨라요? (eo-neu ge deo ppal-la-yo, "which one is faster?").
미국까지 얼마나 걸려요?
mi-guk-kka-ji eol-ma-na geol-lyeo-yo
"How long does it take to get to the U.S.?" To ask for days, say 며칠 걸려요? (myeo-chil geol-lyeo-yo, "how many days?"). The answer is an estimate, since customs time is extra. Numbers like 삼 일 (sam il, three days) are in my number words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel).
추적 번호 알려 주세요
chu-jeok beon-ho al-lyeo ju-se-yo
"Please tell me the tracking number." Korea Post calls tracking 행방조회 (haeng-bang jo-hoe) and the number 등기번호 (deung-gi-beon-ho). The number is on your receipt, so you can also point and say 여기 적어 주세요 (yeo-gi jeo-geo ju-se-yo, "please write it here"). Use it at the Korea Post EMS tracking page; the English Track & Trace page shows the format as EE123456789 KR.
카드로 계산할게요
ka-deu-ro gye-san-hal-ge-yo
"I'll pay by card." Ask first with 카드 돼요? (ka-deu dwae-yo, "can I pay by card?"). If the answer is no, say 현금으로 할게요 (hyeon-geu-meu-ro hal-ge-yo, "I'll pay cash"). Get the receipt with 영수증 주세요 (yeong-su-jeung ju-se-yo, "a receipt, please"). Card words and amounts also appear in my ATM phrases.
