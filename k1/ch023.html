Luggage Lockers at Seoul Station

Voice:
짐 보관함
jim bo-gwan-ham
"Luggage locker." A word you will see on many locker signs; 물품보관함 is the other common one.
보관함이 꽉 찼어요
bo-gwan-ha-mi kkwak cha-sseo-yo
"The lockers are full." What the machine's screen tells you, or what a staff member says while pointing you to another cluster.
큰 사이즈 있어요?
keun sa-i-jeu i-sseo-yo
"Do you have a large size?" Useful at the T-Luggage counter when the machine lockers are all too small.
카드만 돼요
ka-deu-man dwae-yo
"Card only." What a sign or staff member may tell you if a machine does not take cash.
비밀번호를 눌러 주세요
bi-mil-beon-ho-reul nul-leo ju-se-yo
"Please enter the password." What the locker screen displays when it wants your 4-digit PIN.
짐 좀 맡아 주실 수 있어요?
jim jom ma-ta ju-sil su i-sseo-yo
"Could you hold my bag for me?" Only for a staffed counter like T-Luggage. There's no one to ask this at a coin locker.
몇 시까지 찾아야 돼요?
myeot-si-kka-ji cha-ja-ya dwae-yo
"By what time do I need to pick it up?" Ask this if you're not sure your train home leaves before the locker's closing time.
짐 찾으러 왔어요
jim cha-jeu-reo wa-sseo-yo
"I'm here to pick up my bag." Say this if you go to the staffed counter to collect a bag rather than using your own locker code.
