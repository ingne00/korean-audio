Palace and Museum Tickets

Voice:
어른 두 장 주세요
eo-reun du jang ju-se-yo
"Two adult tickets, please." 어른 is "adult" and 장 is the counter for flat things like tickets, used with native numbers: 한 장, 두 장, 세 장. The price board uses the formal word 대인 (dae-in) for the adult fare. On the official fee page, a Gyeongbokgung adult ticket is 3,000 won, and for foreign visitors "adult" means ages 19 to 64. Groups of 10 or more pay 2,400 won each.
통합관람권 한 장 주세요
tong-hap-gwal-lam-gwon han jang ju-se-yo
"One integrated ticket, please." The 궁궐 통합관람권 (combined palace pass) costs 6,000 won and gives one entry each to the four palaces (Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung, Changgyeonggung, Deoksugung) and Jongmyo Shrine within six months of purchase. It does not cover the Secret Garden (후원) at Changdeokgung. You can buy it at the ticket offices of those five sites, and a refund is possible only where you bought it and only if no part has been torn off (fee page).
한복 입으면 무료예요?
han-bok i-beu-myeon mu-ryo-ye-yo
"Is it free if I wear hanbok?" The answer is yes: people wearing hanbok are on the free list, and the hanbok guideline says this applies to foreign visitors as well. The guideline asks for a full outfit: a jacket (저고리) with a wrap collar plus a skirt or trousers. A coat over ordinary clothes, or a hanbok jacket with jeans, does not count.
아이는 무료예요?
a-i-neun mu-ryo-ye-yo
"Is it free for children?" For foreign visitors, entry is free at 18 and under and at 65 and over (fee page). The page says anyone claiming free entry must show proof, so bring passports for the family, including the children's. For a grandparent, ask 65세 이상은 무료예요? (yuk-sip-o-se i-sang-eun mu-ryo-ye-yo, "is it free for 65 and over?").
몇 시까지 들어갈 수 있어요?
myeot si-kka-ji deu-reo-gal su i-sseo-yo
"Until what time can I go in?" Last entry is an hour before closing. The official hours page lists Gyeongbokgung at 09:00 to 17:00 from November to February (last entry 16:00), 09:00 to 18:00 in March to May and September to October (last entry 17:00), and 09:00 to 18:30 from June to August (last entry 17:30).
영어 해설은 몇 시에 있어요?
yeong-eo hae-seo-reun myeot si-e i-sseo-yo
"What time is the English tour?" 해설 is a guided explanation. Gyeongbokgung runs free English tours at 11:00, 13:30 and 15:30 every day it is open, starting in front of the information office just inside Heungnyemun and lasting one to one and a half hours. Groups under 10 can join without booking, and the tour leaves on time (guided tour page).
화요일에도 열어요?
hwa-yo-i-re-do yeo-reo-yo
"Is it open on Tuesdays too?" Gyeongbokgung and Jongmyo close on Tuesdays, while Changdeokgung, Deoksugung and Changgyeonggung close on Mondays. If the closing day falls on a public holiday, the palace opens and closes on the next non-holiday instead (hours page).
