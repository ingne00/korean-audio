The ATM

Voice:
근처에 ATM 어디 있어요?
geun-cheo-e e-i-ti-em eo-di i-sseo-yo
"Where is an ATM nearby?" Koreans say the letters in English style, 에이티엠 (e-i-ti-em). The full word is 현금인출기 (hyeon-geum-in-chul-gi). To ask for a foreign-card machine, say 글로벌 ATM (geul-lo-beol e-i-ti-em, "Global ATM") instead.
카드가 안 나와요
ka-deu-ga an na-wa-yo
"My card won't come out." For a machine that kept it, say 기계가 카드를 먹었어요 (gi-gye-ga ka-deu-reul meo-geo-sseo-yo, "the machine ate my card") and then 도와주세요 (do-wa-ju-se-yo, "please help"). Stay at the machine, tell bank staff or call the number on the ATM, and call your home bank to freeze the card if you cannot get it back.
달러를 원으로 환전하고 싶어요
dal-leo-reul wo-neu-ro hwan-jeon-ha-go si-peo-yo
"I would like to exchange dollars for won." Swap 달러 (dal-leo, dollar) for 유로 (yu-ro, euro) or 엔 (en, yen). At the counter for a cash withdrawal, say 현금을 찾고 싶어요 (hyeon-geu-meul chat-go si-peo-yo, "I would like to withdraw cash"). Have your passport out and say 여권 여기 있어요 (yeo-gwon yeo-gi i-sseo-yo, "here is my passport").
이 기계에서 해외 카드 쓸 수 있어요?
i gi-gye-e-seo hae-oe ka-deu sseul su i-sseo-yo
"Can I use an overseas card at this machine?" If the screen rejected you, say 외국 카드가 안 돼요 (oe-guk ka-deu-ga an dwae-yo, "my foreign card doesn't work") and point at the error.
수수료가 얼마예요?
su-su-ryo-ga eol-ma-ye-yo
"How much is the fee?" 수수료 (su-su-ryo) is the word for a service fee, so it is also the word to look for on the screen before you confirm.
비밀번호가 틀렸다고 나와요
bi-mil-beon-ho-ga teul-lyeot-da-go na-wa-yo
"It says my PIN is wrong." 비밀번호 literally means "secret number", and it is the word for a PIN or password.
영어로 도와주세요
yeong-eo-ro do-wa-ju-se-yo
"Please help me in English." To find a person, say 영어 할 수 있는 분 계세요? (yeong-eo hal su in-neun bun gye-se-yo, "is there someone who speaks English?"). The Korea Tourism Organization's 1330 Travel Helpline (1330 in Korea, +82-2-1330 from overseas) is available in English and other languages and offers interpretation. Check its VisitKorea page for the current hours.
