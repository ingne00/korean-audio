Paying for the Bus

이 버스 강남역 가요?
i beo-seu gang-nam-nyeok ga-yo
Does this bus go to Gangnam Station?
두 명이에요.
du myeong-i-e-yo
There are two of us (paying with one card)
요금이 얼마예요?
yo-geum-i eol-ma-ye-yo
How much is the fare?
도착하면 알려 주세요.
do-cha-ka-myeon al-lyeo ju-se-yo
Please tell me when we arrive
내릴게요.
nae-ril-ge-yo
I'm getting off here
여기 앉아도 돼요?
yeo-gi an-ja-do dwae-yo
Can I sit here?
여기서 갈아타면 돼요?
yeo-gi-seo ga-ra-ta-myeon dwae-yo
Can I transfer here?
