The Library

회원증 만들고 싶어요
hoe-won-jeung man-deul-go si-peo-yo
"I'd like to make a library card."
이 책 대출할게요
i chaek dae-chul-hal-ge-yo
"I'll borrow this book." The ending -ㄹ게요 means "I'll do it", which suits a counter.
반납은 어디서 해요?
ban-na-beun eo-di-seo hae-yo
"Where do I return it?" Literally "Where do I do the return?", which is the natural way to ask it.
이 책 연장돼요?
i chaek yeon-jang-dwae-yo
"Can I extend this book?" 연장돼요 is the polite form of 연장되다, "to be extended", which is written as one word, so 연장돼요? asks "can it be extended?" Spell it 돼요, not 되요.
이 책 어디 있어요?
i chaek eo-di i-sseo-yo
"Where is this book?" Hold up your phone or point at the search screen while you say it. To ask for a room, say 디지털자료실은 어디예요? (di-ji-teol-ja-ryo-si-reun eo-di-ye-yo, "where is the digital materials room?").
자동대출반납기는 어디 있어요?
ja-dong-dae-chul-ban-nap-gi-neun eo-di i-sseo-yo
"Where is the self-checkout machine?" It is long, so the short fallback is to point at a machine and say 이거 어떻게 써요? (i-geo eo-tteo-ke sseo-yo, "how do I use this?").
열람실은 어디예요?
yeol-lam-si-reun eo-di-ye-yo
"Where is the reading room?" 열람 means looking through books and documents, and 실 means room.
