The Nail Salon

Voice:
오늘 예약되나요?
o-neul ye-yak-doe-na-yo
"Can I book a time today?" 예약 is a reservation, and 되나요 asks "does that work?". Add the hour you want; the number words in my time words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel) help.
이 사진처럼 해 주세요
i sa-jin-cheo-reom hae ju-se-yo
"Please do it like this photo." Show the picture on your phone and point to the nails you mean. 처럼 is "like".
기존 젤 제거해 주세요
gi-jon jel je-geo-hae ju-se-yo
"Please remove the gel I have on now." 기존 is "existing" and 제거 is "removal". Say it right after you sit down, before you show the design.
길이는 짧게, 모양은 둥글게 해 주세요
gi-ri-neun jjal-ge, mo-yang-eun dung-geul-ge hae ju-se-yo
"Please make the length short and the shape round." 길이 is length and 모양 is shape. Show the length with your thumb and finger.
젤 알레르기가 있는데 괜찮을까요?
jel al-le-reu-gi-ga in-neun-de gwaen-cha-neul-kka-yo
"I have a gel allergy. Will that be all right?" My suggestion: say it before the first coat, and ask what the shop can do instead.
이제 만져도 돼요?
i-je man-jyeo-do dwae-yo
"Can I touch it now?" Ask before you reach for your bag or phone.
카드로 결제할게요
ka-deu-ro gyeol-je-hal-ge-yo
"I'll pay by card." To ask the total first, say 총 얼마예요? (chong eol-ma-ye-yo). My suggestion: compare it with the statement the shop must give when you take 3 or more services (Gangseo page). For price words, see my shopping words chapter (in Korean Words for Travel).
